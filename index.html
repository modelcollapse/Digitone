Born from

made to be played.

The Digitone distills four decades of FM synthesis research into an instrument you can reach out and touch. Eight voices of pure digital tone generation, each built from four sine-wave operators arranged in eight configurable algorithms.

Where most synthesizers add layers of complexity, the Digitone subtracts. Every parameter has a purpose. Every knob tells a story.

04 — Capabilities

Every detail,
deliberately designed.

LFO Engine

Sine, triangle, square, sawtooth, random, and custom breakpoint LFOs — each assignable to any parameter with per-step retrigger.

Modulation

P-Locks

Lock any parameter to any step. Melody, rhythm, and timbre evolve within a single pattern.

Sequencing

Full MIDI

Web MIDI API integration. Send to any connected hardware — connect your Digitone, Moog, or DAW.

Connectivity

Eight Algorithms

From stacked carriers to complex feedback networks — each algorithm opens a different sonic territory. Stack voices, layer operators, modulate the modulators.

FM Synthesis

Per-Op Envelopes

Attack, decay, sustain, release — independent for each operator. Shape the harmonic character over time.

Envelopes

FX Chain

Chorus, delay, reverb, and overdrive on a dedicated master channel. Sculpt the room around your sound.

05 — Tracks

Four tracks.
Four worlds.

T1

Carrier One

The melodic voice. Assign to MIDI channel 1, map keys from your controller, and let the FM engine do the rest.

T2

Harmonic Layer

Pad textures, chord stabs, or evolving drones. Detune operator B by a few cents and watch the beating begin.

T3

Rhythmic Pulse

High modulation index, short decay — instant percussion. Bass drums, snares, hi-hats, and more from pure FM.

T4

Bass Foundation

Detune operator C to ratio 0.5 for sub frequencies. Add feedback for grit. The underpinning of everything.
