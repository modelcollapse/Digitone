Feature · Astrology

The Grand Conjunction of 2026

Once every twenty years, Jupiter and Saturn meet in the sky — but the 2026 conjunction carries a charge unlike any in living memory. Both giants align at zero degrees Aries, the absolute origin point of the zodiac, signaling not just a reset but a complete dissolution of the old cycle.

This is an era of collective initiation. Nations, institutions, and individuals who have been deferring transformation will find that delay is no longer an option. The universe charges forward, and you are invited — or compelled — to move with it.

Feature · Tarot

Reading the Major Arcana in Modern Life

The 22 cards of the Major Arcana are not relics of superstition. They are a compressed mythology of the human experience — from the Fool's wild leap of faith at 0, to the World's triumphant integration at 21. Every crisis, every love, every identity collapse lives somewhere on this spectrum.

In this feature, three master readers share how they use the Arcana not as prophecy but as a language of reflection — a tool for naming what is already known in the body but not yet said aloud.

Practice · Lunar

Moon Phases & Your Ritual Calendar

Living in alignment with lunar cycles is one of the oldest forms of intelligence. Before electricity fragmented our nights, entire civilizations structured planting, healing, ceremony, and grief around the moon's rhythm. The cycle is still there — we simply stopped watching.

This guide maps the eight moon phases to eight modes of inner work. From the quiet of the new moon to the raw release of the waning crescent, every phase asks something different of you.
